## A2. HOOK ANALYSIS — WHY YOUR HOOKS AREN'T LANDING

**Problem diagnosed:** Your current hooks are "topic introductions" — they tell the viewer WHAT the video is about. That's not a hook. A hook creates TENSION in the first 3 seconds.

**The fix — 3 rules for every hook going forward:**

| Rule | Wrong | Right |
|------|-------|-------|
| 1. Start mid-story | "Aaj hum wildlife sanctuaries padhenge" | "Ek student ne mujhse pucha — sir 2024 mein Govind Sanctuary ka answer 1955 likha, 2025 mein galat ho gaya. Kyun?" |
| 2. Specific number first | "Important topics discuss karenge" | "45 facts. Har ek UKPSC mein puchha gaya. Full list aaj de raha hoon." |
| 3. Challenge/dare | "Yeh topics padh lo" | "Agar tum yeh 45 facts nahi jaante — toh Prelims mein 15-20 marks guaranteed jayenge" |
