## THUMBNAIL RULES FOR YOUR CHANNEL:

Based on what works in your niche:

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| Face thumbnails > No face | Education niche = trust = face needed 70% of time |
| Maximum 4-5 words on thumbnail | More text = harder to read on phone |
| ONE dominant emotion | Confused / Shocked / Confident — pick ONE per thumbnail |
| Contrast: Dark BG + bright text (or vice versa) | Stands out in feed |
| Red/Yellow for urgency, Blue/Navy for authority, Green for positive | Color psychology |
| Never put the full title on thumbnail | Thumbnail + Title = complement each other, don't repeat |
