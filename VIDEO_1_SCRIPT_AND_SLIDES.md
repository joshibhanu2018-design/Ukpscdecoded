## A. VIDEO OVERVIEW

**Hook/Angle:** UKPSC doesn't just repeat TOPICS — it repeats exact QUESTION FORMATS. The same "match the following" structure, the same "arrange in ascending order" format, the same entity-confusion trap — year after year. Students memorize facts but miss the pattern hiding in plain sight.

**Core Insight:** Across 4 papers (2016-2025), UKPSC has 8 confirmed cross-year repeats and at least 5 recurring question FORMATS that appear every single year regardless of which specific fact they test. Knowing the format = knowing what to prepare even before the paper is set.

**Reserved for Later Videos (DO NOT USE in Video 1):**
- The 12-question dynasty/inscription cluster density in a single paper (save for Video 2 — History deep-dive)
- District comparison FORMAT trap — the stat changes but structure stays identical (save for Video 3 or standalone)
- UKPSC Current Affairs ≠ UPSC Current Affairs — state schemes/budget dominate, not national events (save for Video 5/6)
