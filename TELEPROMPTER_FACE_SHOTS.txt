Ab sabse important baat — cut-off prediction ke liye hume dekhna padta hai previous years ka data.

Dekho screen par — pichhle RO/ARO Prelims ki official cut-offs:

General category — RO Secretariat mein 98 marks, RO UKPSC mein 97 marks.

OBC — 97.75 aur 96.25 marks respectively.

EWS — lagbhag 94.50 ke aaspaas.

SC — 87 se 85.75 ke beech.

ST — 88.50 marks.

Uttarakhand Mahila — 85 se 88 ke range mein.

Toh historically, General cut-off 60 se 65 percent ke aaspaas hoti hai screening ke liye. Yeh benchmark hai.

Ab — aaj ka paper agar TOUGHER tha previous years se, toh cut-off NEECHE jayegi. Agar EASIER tha, toh UPAR jayegi. Simple logic.
