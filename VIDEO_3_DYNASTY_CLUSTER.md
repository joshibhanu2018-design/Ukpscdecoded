**Hook Angle:** The DENSITY of dynasty questions in a single paper is abnormal — 12 out of ~50 Uttarakhand questions come from just 3 ruling families. Nobody teaches them as a connected timeline. Students study them as isolated facts and get confused when cross-dynasty questions appear.

**Core Insight:** Katyuri → Parmar (Garhwal) + Chand (Kumaon) is ONE continuous political story. If you understand the TIMELINE (not isolated facts), you can answer any angle UKPSC throws at you — coins, inscriptions, temples, capitals, rulers, or events.
