## 5.1 FOUNDATION OF TEHRI STATE

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| **Context** | After British defeated Gorkhas (1815), territory was divided |
| **Division** | Alaknanda River as boundary |
| **British Territory** | East of Alaknanda → Kumaon Commissionerate |
| **Returned to Parmar King** | West of Alaknanda (excluding Dehradun & Rawain) |
| **Founder** | **Sudarshan Shah** (son of martyred Pradyumna Shah) |
| **Date of Foundation** | **December 28, 1815** |
| **Capital Location** | Confluence of **Bhagirathi and Bhilangana** rivers |
| **Name Origin** | 'Tehri' derived from 'Trihari' — a place that washes away three types of sins (Mansa, Vacha, Karmana) |
| **British Treaty** | March 4, 1820 — Sudarshan Shah signed treaty with British to secure dynasty |

---

## 5.2 KEY MONARCHS — CHRONOLOGICAL TABLE

| Ruler | Period | Key Epithet | Major Contributions & Events |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sudarshan Shah** | 1815–1859 | Founder; Poet-King | Founded capital at Tehri (Dec 28, 1815); signed British treaty (March 4, 1820); built **'Purana Darbar' palace** (1848); authored 7-volume **Sabhasar Granth** under pen name **'Surat Kavi'** |
| **Bhawani Shah** | 1859–1871 | — | Consolidation period |
| **Pratap Shah** | 1871–1886 | The Great Reformist | Founded **Pratapnagar** (1877); introduced English education; first police department; **first hospital** (1883); **Pratap Printing Press**; started the newspaper *Garhwali* |
| **Kirti Shah** | 1886–1913 | Knight Commander | Brought **electricity** to Tehri; established **Farmer's Bank**; invented **Hindi typewriter**; organized **Sarvdharma Sammelan** (1902); sponsored Swami Ram Tirth's visit to Japan; conferred 'Companion of India' & 'Knight Commander' by British (1901) |
| **Narendra Shah** | 1913–1946 | Builder-King (but controversial) | Founded **Narendranagar** (1921); shifted capital there (1925); massive infrastructure growth; **Narendra Hindu Law** compiled (1918 by Harikrishna Raturi); **Tilari Massacre** (1930); **Sridev Suman's martyrdom** (1944) |
| **Manvendra Shah** | 1946–1949 | Last King | Signed merger document (May 18, 1948); Tehri officially merged with Indian Union on **August 1, 1949** |

## 5.3 ADMINISTRATIVE STRUCTURE

### The King's Title & Status

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| **Title** | **Bolanda Badrish** (Speaking Badrinath) — King revered as incarnation of God |
| **Authority** | Supreme, absolute |
| **Nature** | Feudal monarchy under British paramountcy |

### Central Council (Council of Ministers)

| Position | Role |
|---|---|
| **Mukhtaar** | Chief Minister |
| **Wazir / Diwan** | Council of Ministers; handled policy & military |
| **Bakshi** | Messenger / Dispatcher |
| **Daftari** | In-charge of Royal Orders |

### Territorial Division

```
STATE (King — Bolanda Badrish)
    ↓
THANO (headed by THANEDAR)
    ↓
PARGANAS / MANDALS (headed by FAUJDAR or Supervisor)
    ↓
PATTIS (headed by KANUNGO)
    ↓
VILLAGES (managed by PATWARI and PRADHAN)
```

### Judicial System

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| **Supreme Authority** | King (final arbiter) |
| **Codified Law** | **Narendra Hindu Law** — compiled in **1918** by **Pandit Harikrishna Raturi** (published 1917) |
| **Significance** | First formal codification of Hindu personal law in a princely state of the region |
| **Raturi's Other Contribution** | Also authored *Garhwal ka Itihas* (History of Garhwal) — a foundational historical text |

## 5.4 ECONOMY & TAXATION

### Land Ownership

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| **Supreme Owner** | The King |
| **Land Classification** | Same as Parmar era |

### Land Grant Types

| Type | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Vishnupriti** | Tax-free land donated to Brahmins or temples |
| **Raut** | Land awarded to soldiers for bravery |
| **Jagir** | Land given to state officials instead of salary |

### Major Taxes

| Tax | Description |
|---|---|
| **Tihar** | Primary agricultural tax — **1/3 of total production** |
| **Sirti** | Main cash income source |
| **Bhumikar** | Land tax (cash) |
| **Punituti / Paud Tuti** | Import-export tax on goods crossing state borders |
| **Bara-Begar** | Deeply exploitative forced unpaid labour exacted by officials |

### Trade System

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| **Dhakari System** | Barter arrangement — villagers exchanged ghee, oil, spices for cloth, salt, jaggery |
| **Trans-Himalayan Trade** | Traders from Niti and Mana valleys actively traded with Tibet |
| **Muafidars** | Revenue-free grantees — part of oppressive bureaucratic class |

## 5.5 SOCIETY, RELIGION & CULTURE

### Social Structure

- Deep divide between **oppressive monarchical bureaucracy** (including muafidars) and **suffering peasantry**
- Forced labour (Begar) was a major source of social tension
- Rural society dominated by traditional caste hierarchies

### Religious Patronage

| Initiative | Details |
|---|---|
| **Sarvdharma Sammelan (1902)** | Interfaith conference organized by Kirti Shah |
| **Swami Ram Tirth** | Sponsored by Kirti Shah to attend international religious conference in Japan |
| **Key Temples** | Surkanda Devi, Chandrabadni, Kunjapuri |
| **Naga Worship** | Sem Mukhem Nagraja Temple — signifies deep roots of Naga worship |

### Art & Literature

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| **Garhwal School of Painting** | Kept alive; prominent artists: **Chaitu and Manku** |
| **Sudarshan Shah as Poet** | 7-volume **Sabhasar Granth** under pen name 'Surat Kavi' |
| **Harikrishna Raturi** | Narendra Hindu Law (1918) + *Garhwal ka Itihas* |

### Growth of Vernacular Journalism

| Newspaper | Year | Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Garhwali** | 1905 | First published from Dehradun; first editor: **Girija Dutt Naithani**; mouthpiece of Garhwal Union; focused on social reforms |
| **Shakti** | Later | Important newspaper in the history of Uttarakhand journalism |
| **Pratap Printing Press** | 1880s | Established by Pratap Shah — enabled local publishing |

## 5.6 POPULAR MOVEMENTS AGAINST THE MONARCHY

### Timeline of Anti-Monarchy Movements

| Movement / Event | Year | Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Athoor Rebellion** | 1851 | Led by **Badri Singh Aswal** against cruel collection of Tihar tax |
| **Khas Patti / Kunjdi Van Movement** | 1904–1906 | Against Conservator Keshavanand's attempt to reserve forests around Chandrabadni temple |
| **Tilari / Rawain Massacre** | May 30, 1930 | **THE MOST IMPORTANT EVENT** — detailed below |
| **Tehri Rajya Praja Mandal** | January 23, 1939 | Founded in Dehradun by **Sridev Suman** and others |
| **Sridev Suman's Martyrdom** | July 25, 1944 | 84-day fast-unto-death in Tehri jail |
| **Saklana Revolt** | 1947 | **Nagendra Saklani** martyred (Jan 11, 1948) |
| **Kirtinagar Revolt** | January 1948 | Revolutionaries including **Moluram** martyred |
| **Merger Agreement Signed** | May 18, 1948 | By last king Manvendra Shah |
| **Official Merger** | **August 1, 1949** | Tehri merged into United Provinces (UP) as independent district |

## 5.7 THE TILARI / RAWAIN MASSACRE (May 30, 1930)

> **THE JALLIANWALA BAGH OF UTTARAKHAND**

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New forest laws (1927–28) under Narendra Shah — restricted traditional grazing rights, fodder collection, fuelwood, community festivals |
| **Public Response** | Formation of **'Azad Panchayat'**; farmers gathered for unarmed protest meeting |
| **Location** | **Tiladi ground** on banks of Yamuna River |
| **Ordered Firing** | Tehri's Diwan **Chakradhar Juyal** |
| **Nature** | Indiscriminate firing on unarmed crowds |
| **Historical Comparison** | Called **"Jallianwala Bagh of Uttarakhand"** / "Jallianwala Bagh of Garhwal" |
| **Chakradhar Juyal Called** | **"General Dyer of Tehri"** |
| **Commemoration** | **May 30 = Martyr's Day** (observed to this day) |

## 5.8 TEHRI RAJYA PRAJA MANDAL & SRIDEV SUMAN

### Praja Mandal

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| **Founded** | **January 23, 1939** |
| **Location** | Dehradun |
| **Founded By** | **Sridev Suman** and associates |
| **Objective** | Demand a responsible, democratic government under the King's patronage |
| **Inspiration** | Gandhian methods of non-violent protest |

### Sridev Suman — The Supreme Martyr

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| **Full Name** | Sridev Suman |
| **Ideology** | Gandhian non-violence; staunch admirer of Gandhi |
| **Arrest** | Arrested for activism against Tehri monarchy |
| **Fast Duration** | **84-day fast-unto-death** in Tehri jail |
| **Date of Martyrdom** | **July 25, 1944** |
| **Impact** | His death catalyzed mass rebellions — Saklana Revolt (1947) & Kirtinagar Revolt (1948) |
| **Legacy** | University named after him (Sridev Suman Uttarakhand University); considered the greatest martyr of Uttarakhand's princely state era |

## 5.9 THE FINAL REVOLTS & MERGER

### Saklana Revolt (1947)

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| **Leader** | **Nagendra Saklani** (Communist Party of India leader) |
| **Context** | Peasant uprising; supported by Dada Daulat Ram |
| **Martyrdom** | Nagendra Saklani killed **January 11, 1948** while defending liberated Kirtinagar division from royal forces |
| **Significance** | CPI's role in Tehri liberation movement |

### Kirtinagar Revolt (January 1948)

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| **Context** | Revolutionaries seized Kirtinagar division |
| **Martyrs** | **Moluram** and others |
| **Royal Response** | King's forces attempted retake |
| **Result** | Immense pressure forced the monarchy to concede |

### The Merger

| Step | Date | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Merger Agreement Signed | **May 18, 1948** | Last king **Manvendra Shah** signed under immense public & political pressure |
| **Official Merger** | **August 1, 1949** | Tehri State legally dissolved; merged into **United Provinces (Uttar Pradesh)** as independent district |
| **Legal Instrument** | Instrument of Accession — "The Maharaja of Tehri-Garhwal hereby cedes to the Dominion Government full and exclusive authority..." |

> **KEY FACT:** Tehri was one of the last princely states to merge with India (August 1, 1949 — over 2 years after independence).
