## 21.1 OVERVIEW: THE UTTARAKHAND ECONOMIC MODEL

Uttarakhand's economy has undergone a **26-fold transformation** since its formation on 9 November 2000, transitioning from a subsistence-based "Money Order Economy" (dependent on remittances from migrant soldiers and workers) to a diversified **Himalayan growth model** driven by manufacturing, tourism, and services.

| Parameter | At Formation (2000-01) | Current (2025-26) | Growth Multiple |
|---|---|---|---|
| **GSDP (Current Prices)** | ~₹14,786 Crore | **₹3,81,889 Crore** | **~26×** |
| **Per Capita Income** | ~₹15,000 | **₹2,73,921** | **~18×** |
| **Tax Revenue** | ₹233 Crore | **₹11,862 Crore** | **~51×** |
| **Budget Size** | ~₹5,000 Crore | **₹1,11,703 Crore** | **~22×** |
| **MSME Units** | 14,163 | **79,394–94,595** | **~6×** |
| **Exports** | Negligible | **₹14,952 Crore** | — |
| **Startups** | 0 | **1,100 recognized** | — |
| **Electrification** | 60% | **100%** | — |

### Key Characteristics of the Economy

1. **Service-Sector Dominance:** Over 50% of GSDP from the Tertiary sector (tourism, trade, transport)
2. **Manufacturing Corridor in Plains:** Secondary sector concentrated in Haridwar-US Nagar-Dehradun belt (SIDCUL IIEs)
3. **Primary Sector Crisis:** Agriculture contributes <10% to GSDP despite employing ~58% of the rural workforce
4. **High Per Capita Income:** Consistently above national average due to small population + industrial output
5. **Ecological Accounting Pioneer:** First state globally to index Gross Environmental Product (GEP)
6. **Tourism Dependence:** ~7 Crore annual tourists for a ~1 Crore population; tourism contributes 9% directly and 15% indirectly to GSDP
7. **Dual Economy (Hills vs Plains):** 86% of industrial output from 2 plain districts; hill districts face capital flight and out-migration

## 21.4 SECTORAL CONTRIBUTION TO GSDP (2025-26 Estimates)

| Sector | Contribution (%) | Growth Rate (%) | Key Sub-Sectors |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | **9.59%** | 2.06% (sluggish) | Agriculture (33.50%), Livestock (27.25%), Forestry (27.04%) |
| **Secondary** | **39.95%** | 7.98% | Manufacturing (65.68%), Construction (26.45%) |
| **Tertiary** | **50.46%** | **11.11%** (fastest) | Trade/Hotels/Restaurants (37.25%), Transport/Communication (18.16%) |

### The Primary Sector Crisis

| Sub-Sector | Share within Primary | Growth |
|---|---|---|
| Agriculture | 33.50% | **-3.96% (NEGATIVE)** |
| Livestock | 27.25% | **+5.41%** |
| Forestry & Logging | 27.04% | Stable |
| Fishing | ~2% | Growing |

> **Critical Point:** While 58% of the rural workforce depends on farming, it contributes only ~3.2% to GSDP. This **"Primary Sector Paradox"** — high employment, low output — is the root cause of migration and rural distress.

### Structural Shift Over 25 Years

| Sector | 2000-01 (%) | 2025-26 (%) | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | ~28% | 9.59% | ↓ 18 points |
| Secondary | ~27% | 39.95% | ↑ 13 points |
| Tertiary | ~45% | 50.46% | ↑ 5 points |

## 21.7 ECONOMIC REFORMS SINCE STATE FORMATION (2000–2026)

### Phase 1: Industrial Foundation (2000–2005)

| Reform | Details |
|---|---|
| **SIDCUL (2002)** | State Infrastructure & Industrial Development Corporation; 7 IIEs built |
| **Special Federal Package (2003)** | 10-year central excise + income tax holiday; attracted FMCG/Pharma/Auto giants |
| **Special Category Status** | Granted 2 May 2001 — provided 90:10 Centre:State funding ratio |

### Phase 2: Fiscal Modernization (2005–2017)

| Reform | Details |
|---|---|
| **FRBM Act (2005)** | Fiscal Responsibility & Budget Management; caps deficit at 3% of GSDP |
| **VAT (April 2005)** | Value Added Tax replaced outdated sales tax |
| **GST (July 2017)** | 2,22,461 new registrations; unified market; ₹7,301 Crore SGST |
| **Revenue Police → Regular Police** | Phased transition from colonial-era dual system |

### Phase 3: Digital & Governance Revolution (2017–2025)

| Reform | Details |
|---|---|
| **Apni Sarkar Portal** | 954 services, 78 departments; 1 Crore+ applications; 99% processing rate |
| **Single Window (investuttarakhand.com)** | "Top Achiever" in BRAP 2024 |
| **Devbhoomi Parivar Yojana** | 360° family database; proactive benefit push |
| **Digital Forensic Lab** | AI-integrated tax compliance in Dehradun |
| **Bill Lao Inam Pao (BLIPUK App)** | Gamified tax enforcement via consumers |
| **e-Office, e-Bhulekh, e-RCMS** | Paperless administration |

### Phase 4: Landmark Legal Reforms (2025–2026)

| Reform | Details |
|---|---|
| **Uniform Civil Code (27 Jan 2025)** | First state in India; equal property rights for daughters |
| **Anti-Copying Law** | Life imprisonment for paper leaks |
| **Bhu-Kanoon (2025)** | Outsiders banned from agricultural land purchase in 11 hill districts |
| **Religious Freedom Amendment (2025)** | 3–10 years for forced conversion |
| **Gross Environmental Product (GEP)** | First state globally to index ecological value alongside GSDP |
| **Green Hydrogen Policy (2026)** | Target: 100 kilotonnes by 2030 |
| **Geothermal Energy Policy (2025)** | UREDA + UJVNL-led exploration |
