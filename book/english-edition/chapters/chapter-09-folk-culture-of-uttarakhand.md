## PART A: FOLK DANCES OF UTTARAKHAND

> *Folk music is the "heartbeat of the culture" — a profound manifestation of regional identity.*

---

## 9.1 MARTIAL AND WAR-STYLE DANCES

| Dance | Region/Tribe | Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Chholia Dance** | Kumaon | War-style dance with **sword and shield**; characteristic of wedding processions; vigorous movements |
| **Saron Dance** | Garhwal | Garhwal's equivalent of martial dance; with swords and shields to rhythm of drums |
| **Pauna (Pona) Dance** | Bhotiya tribe | Similar to Saron style; performed during weddings and auspicious occasions; often holding a handkerchief |

---

## 9.2 RELIGIOUS AND RITUALISTIC DANCES

| Dance | Region | Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Ramman** | Saloor-Dungra villages, Chamoli | Globally recognized folk cultural festival & ritual theater; **UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage (2009)**; features Ramayana stories, mask dances, rituals for deity **Bhumiyal Devta**; celebrated annually in April-May (Baisakh) |
| **Pandav Nritya** | Kedar Valley, Garhwal | Religious dance-drama narrating **Mahabharata** story; involves singing, dancing, recitation |
| **Jaggar (Jagad) Dance** | Both Garhwal & Kumaon | Major dance to invoke deities; involves three characters: **Jagarya** (singer), **Oji** (instrumentalist), **Paswa** (medium upon whom deity descends) |
| **Ran Bhoot (Devta Ghirana)** | — | Organized to appease and worship souls of those martyred on battlefield |
| **Bagadwal Dance** | Central Garhwal | Performed to pray for happiness and prosperity |

---


## 9.3 ORNAMENTAL AND SOCIAL DANCES

| Dance | Region | Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Chonfla (Chaufla)** | Both regions | Ornamental dance categorized alongside India's Bihu and Garba; men and women dance in circle **without musical instruments**; rely on collective singing |
| **Jhora (Kumaon) / Chanchari (Garhwal)** | Kumaon & Garhwal | Ornamental circle dances; participants place hands on each other's shoulders; lead singer plays **Hudki** in centre; Kumaon: marks arrival of spring; Garhwal: performed on moonlit nights in Srinagar |
| **Thadiya Dance** | Both regions | Performed in courtyard (Thad) when newly married girls return to parental home first time; traditionally from **Basant Panchami to Bikhoti Sankranti** |
| **Jhumailo Dance** | Garhwal | Performed by women (often newly married); reflects beauty and pain of woman's heart; memories of maternal home |
| **Chhopati Dance** | Rawain Jaunpur, Tehri | Popular ornamental dance specific to this region |
| **Tandi Dance** | Garhwal | Songs mention contemporary events and deeds of famous figures |

## 9.17 KEY FACTS ABOUT INSTRUMENTS & CULTURAL CONTEXT

| Fact | Details |
|---|---|
| **Auji Community** | Traditional expertise of playing Dhol and Damau rests primarily with Auji people |
