## 25.1 POVERTY — MULTIDIMENSIONAL POVERTY INDEX (MPI)

### State-Level Progress

| Parameter | Data |
|---|---|
| **MPI Headcount (2024-25)** | **6.92%** (down from 9.7% in 2021-22) |
| **Historical Comparison** | Over 40% at formation → now below 10% |
| **SDG India Index** | **Rank 1** (tied with Kerala) |
| **National MPI Rank** | Among best-performing states |

### The Hill-Plain Poverty Divide

| Category | Districts | Characteristics |
|---|---|---|
| **High Deprivation** | Haridwar (urban slum poverty), Almora (rural mountain blocks) | Nutrition gaps, fuel deprivation |
| **Low Deprivation** | Dehradun (HDI 0.765), Nainital | Service jobs, tourism, state capital spending |

### Core Deprivation Metrics (MPI Dimensions)

| Dimension | Data | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| **Nutrition** | 32% rural hill households experience deficits | Erratic fresh food supply in mountains |
| **Clean Fuel** | 28% high-altitude HHs rely on biomass | LPG cylinder transport cost prohibitive |
| **Sanitation/Housing** | 14% deprivation in basic housing | Forest laws restrict building material access |
| **Education** | Improving but hill schools face single-digit enrolment | Migration-driven demand collapse |

### MAINS ANALYSIS — Poverty

> **Key Insight:** Uttarakhand's poverty is not caloric starvation but **structural multi-dimensional deprivation** — nutrition logistics failure, energy access gaps, and terrain-induced isolation. Standard income metrics understate the real vulnerability of hill communities.

## 25.2 MIGRATION — THE ECONOMIC DRAIN

### Scale of the Crisis

| Parameter | Data |
|---|---|
| **Ghost Villages (zero population)** | **1,100+** completely abandoned |
| **Villages with >50% population drop** | 3,000+ |
| **Daily migration (2018-22)** | 193 people/day |
| **Households with migrants** | 1 in 3 (27.8%; rises to 34.3% in hills) |
| **Primary driver** | Employment search (38.93%) |
| **Remittances** | 75.5% of migrants send money home |

### The "Hollowing-Out" Economic Cycle

```
Out-Migration of Working-Age Youth
        ↓
Abandoned Terraces & Soil Erosion
        ↓
Drop in Local Tax Base & Productivity
        ↓
Money-Order Economy (High Local Inflation)
        ↓
Further Out-Migration (cycle reinforces)
```

### Economic Consequences

| Impact | Details |
|---|---|
| **Remittance Trap** | Cash goes to consumption, not local capital creation → inflation without growth |
| **Farm Abandonment** | Topsoil erosion + Lantana overgrowth + increased HWC |
| **Plain-Bound Overcrowding** | Land prices surge in Haldwani/Dehradun; public services at capacity |
| **Feminization of Agriculture** | Women = 60% of agri labour; 72% of water collection |
| **Strategic Vacuum** | Border districts depopulating → national security risk |
| **Political Marginalization** | Hills losing demographic weight → future delimitation threat |

## 25.3 NATURAL DISASTER — ECONOMIC COST

### Disaster Profile 2025

| Parameter | Data |
|---|---|
| **Total Economic Loss (2025 PDNA)** | **₹15,103.52 Crore** |
| **Recovery & Reconstruction Cost** | ₹10,999 Crore (73% of total) |
| **Direct Asset Damage** | ₹3,792.4 Crore (roads, bridges, power lines, private property) |
| **Human Toll (2025)** | 147 lives lost; 163 injured |
| **Livestock Loss** | 7,145 animals (6,861 small + 284 large) |
| **Roads Blocked (Peak Monsoon)** | 76 |
| **Families Rehabilitated (since 2012)** | 2,985 |
| **Rehabilitation (2025-26)** | 39 families; ₹21.15 Crore |

### Sectoral Impact

| Sector | Impact |
|---|---|
| **Infrastructure (PWD)** | Recurring repair bills; 3-4× maintenance cost vs plains |
| **Tourism** | Helicopter crashes + landslides during Char Dham → revenue disruption |
| **Agriculture** | Crop destruction; livestock death; livelihood loss |
| **Energy** | Hydro turbines damaged by siltation; transmission lines cut |

### The Reconstruction Trap

> **MAINS INSIGHT:** Every major disaster forces the state to redirect development CapEx into emergency reconstruction. This creates a "treadmill effect" — building, destroying, rebuilding — that prevents long-term asset accumulation in hill districts.

## 25.4 ENVIRONMENTAL DEGRADATION — ECONOMIC LOSS

### The "Hidden" Cost

| Parameter | Data |
|---|---|
| **Forest Fire Loss/Hectare** | **₹1,30,387** (mean; actual losses 65-90× higher than official estimates) |
| **Loss Breakdown** | Timber 59%, Tree Regeneration 15%, Fuelwood/Fodder 10%, Wild Edibles 7% |
| **Kedarnath WLS Ecosystem Value** | Dropped from ~$134M (2001) → ~$113.68M (2024) |
| **Terai Arc Landscape Services** | ₹390 Billion (USD 6B) annually — threatened by industrial expansion |
| **Forest Land Diverted (since 2000)** | ~45,000 hectares for non-forest uses |
| **Pirul (Pine Needle) Annual Production** | 23 Lakh MT (major fire catalyst) |

### Key Environmental Threats

| Threat | Economic Implication |
|---|---|
| **Glacial Retreat** | Long-term hydropower potential threatened; Pindari retreating 8-10m/year |
| **Spring Crisis** | 50%+ springs drying → women's burden increases; agri-productivity drops |
| **Invasive Species** | Lantana (1,334 sq km) provides wildlife cover → HWC increases |
| **Forest Fires** | 10,000+ sq km highly vulnerable; destroys regeneration capacity |
| **Carrying Capacity Breach** | Tourism pressure (7 Cr tourists/1 Cr population) → lake eutrophication |

## 25.5 HUMAN-WILDLIFE CONFLICT — THE MIGRATION-HWC FEEDBACK LOOP

### The Destructive Cycle

```
Declining Agrarian Capital (migration + reduced crop protection)
        ↓
Ecosystem Degradation (fallow land + invasive weed overgrowth)
        ↓
Extension of Wildlife Foraging Zones (animals approach villages)
        ↓
Severe Crop Raids & Loss of Human Life
        ↓
Total Abandonment of Agriculture (further migration)
        ↓ (cycle reinforces)
```

### Key Mechanisms

| Mechanism | Impact |
|---|---|
| **Fallow Land as Wildlife Corridors** | Abandoned fields → Lantana cover → wild boar/leopard habitat |
| **Loss of Community Guarding** | Elderly cannot maintain night-vigils; crops become easy targets |
| **Crop Shift to Cash Crops** | Potatoes/peas attract wildlife more than traditional millets |
| **Drying Springs** | Animals leave forests seeking water → enter villages |

### Economic Consequences

| Impact | Details |
|---|---|
| **Compensation raised** | ₹6 Lakh → **₹10 Lakh** for death in HWC |
| **Crop destruction** | Single boar raid can destroy entire season's potato crop |
| **Sunk fencing costs** | Ineffective barriers drain household savings |
| **Abandonment trigger** | Repeated losses → farming abandoned → Ghost Village formation |

### Solutions (Strategic)

| Solution | Details |
|---|---|
| **Bio-Shield Barriers** | Lemongrass, ginger, garlic borders — wildlife-resistant + income-generating |
| **Solar Fencing (Community)** | Youth cooperatives maintain solar-powered smart fencing |
| **Invasive Scrub Clearance** | Use VB-G RAM G funds to clear Lantana → remove wildlife cover |
| **Transit Centres (CAMPA)** | ₹14 Crore for 7 centres (Pauri, Tehri, Uttarkashi, Chamoli, Dehradun, US Nagar, Bageshwar) |
| **Animal Rescue Centres** | ₹5 Crore under CAMPA |

## 25.12 STRUCTURAL WAY FORWARD — INTEGRATED SOLUTIONS

| Solution | Implementation |
|---|---|
| **Value-Added Mountain Hubs** | Solar-powered food processing + cold storage inside hill clusters |
| **Payment for Ecosystem Services (PES)** | National fiscal rewards for preserving Himalayan carbon sinks |
| **Climate-Resilient Infrastructure** | Restrict heavy construction on fragile slopes; updated building codes |
| **Bio-Shield Agriculture** | Lemongrass/aromatic borders → wildlife-resistant + income |
| **Green Tourism Cess** | Revenue locked into climate adaptation fund |
| **Altitude-Based Tax Incentives** | Incentivize homestays/processing above 1,500m |
| **Digital PDS + Parivar ID** | Eliminate ghost beneficiaries; enable migrant portability |
| **SANTULAN Model** | Balance Economy ↔ Ecology for "Carrying Capacity Development" |
