### The CD Ratio Paradox — CRITICAL MAINS ANALYSIS

| Parameter | Data |
|---|---|
| **Overall CD Ratio** | 54.67% (incl. RIDF) |
| **Highest** | US Nagar: **119.42%** |
| **Lowest** | Bageshwar: **24.66%**, Almora: 26.97%, Pauri: 27.47% |

> **Analytical Insight for Mains:** The CD Ratio asymmetry reveals **"Capital Flight"** — savings deposited by hill residents are diverted by banks to fund loans in the plains or outside the state. This structural issue starves hill districts of entrepreneurial investment, fuels Palaayan, and creates a vicious cycle where lack of credit → lack of enterprise → lack of youth → further lack of demand for credit.

### Way Forward (Banking)

- **Digital Banking Units (DBUs):** Paperless units in every block
- **Bank-on-Wheels:** Mobile ATMs for disaster-affected and remote areas
- **Green Credit Framework:** Linking finance to GEP goals
- **District-specific credit targets:** Mandating hill-capital reinvestment into horticulture, trout, and MAPs
- **100% PACS computerization** already achieved → next step: CBS integration

| Issue | Analysis |
|---|---|

| **Siltation** | Glacial melt + landslides reduce turbine lifespan |
| **Way Forward** | Micro-Hydel + Solar + Pumped Storage Plants (PSP) as "Green Batteries"; CEIA for entire river basins; smart meters for 14.57% losses |
