## 24.1 OVERVIEW: FISCAL EVOLUTION SINCE STATEHOOD

Uttarakhand's fiscal journey since 2000 represents a transformation from a revenue-deficit, grant-dependent hill state to a **revenue-surplus economy** with growing self-reliance.

| Parameter | 2000-01 | 2026-27 (BE) | Growth |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Budget Size** | ~₹5,000 Cr | **₹1,11,703 Cr** | **~22×** |
| **Own Tax Revenue** | ₹233 Cr | **₹25,913 Cr** | **~51×** (in 2024-25: ₹11,862 Cr) |
| **Revenue Balance** | Deficit | **Surplus ₹2,536 Cr** | Structural shift |
| **Fiscal Deficit** | High | **2.94% of GSDP** | Within FRBM |
| **Capital Expenditure** | ~₹1,000 Cr | **₹14,763 Cr** (record) | ~15× |
| **GST Dealers** | N/A | **2,79,043** | 2,22,461 new post-2017 |

> **Key Milestone:** The Budget crossed ₹1 Lakh Crore for the **first time** in 2026-27.

## 24.5 THE "COMMITTED EXPENDITURE" CRISIS — CRITICAL ANALYSIS

### The Core Problem

| Component | Amount (₹ Cr) | % of Revenue Receipts |
|---|---|---|
| **Salaries** | ~22,105 | **33%** |
| **Pensions** | ~11,137 | **16%** |
| **Interest Payments** | ~7,929 | **12%** |
| **TOTAL COMMITTED** | **~41,172** | **61%** |

> **MAINS INSIGHT:** With 61% of revenue receipts consumed by non-discretionary, recurring expenses, the state has only **39% fiscal space** for actual development, infrastructure, and welfare.

### Analytical Implications

| Issue | Impact |
|---|---|
| **Compressed Development Space** | Limited ability to scale health, education, and ecological infrastructure |
| **Rising Pension Burden** | Early retirement + large post-2000 recruitment wave → pension explosion |
| **Interest Trap** | Debt servicing (₹28,161 Cr repayment + ₹7,929 Cr interest) = massive annual outflow |
| **National Comparison** | Central Govt allocates 4.4% of GDP to CapEx; UK's CapEx-to-Revenue ratio is better but constrained by committed load |

### Way Forward

- Restructure pension frameworks toward contribution-based models
- Freeze non-essential department expansions
- Utilize multi-tasking administrative cadres
- Channel revenue surplus (₹2,536 Cr) into liquid investment reserves

## 24.11 STRUCTURAL ISSUES — MAINS CRITICAL ANALYSIS

### Issue 1: Excessive Committed Expenditure (Fiscal Rigidity)

**The Problem:** Salaries (₹22,105 Cr) + Pensions (₹11,137 Cr) + Interest (₹7,929 Cr) = **₹41,172 Crore** → locks **61%** of revenue receipts.

**Implication:** Compresses fiscal space for asset creation, health, ecology, and disaster resilience. Any revenue shortfall immediately threatens development spending.

### Issue 2: Fiscal Deficit Trajectory

**The Problem:** Fiscal deficit at 2.94% (2026-27) is close to the 3% FRBM ceiling. Previous year revised estimate was 3.62%. Debt repayment mandate of ₹28,161 Cr adds pressure.

**Implication:** In a disaster year (cloudburst/earthquake), emergency borrowing could breach FRBM — requiring special dispensation.

### Issue 3: Depleting Cash Reserves & Central Dependency

**The Problem:** State Finance Audit shows 100% drawdown in Cash Balance Investment Account. 53% of revenue comes from Central devolution and grants.

**Implication:** Limited independent liquidity buffers for internal shocks. Vulnerable to any delay or reduction in Central transfers.

### Issue 4: The Hill-Plain Development Divide

**The Problem:** CapEx prioritizes transit projects in plain-adjacent zones. Hill districts face 40% post-harvest loss (no cold-chain), low CD Ratio (25%), and continued ghost-village formation.

**Implication:** Fiscal allocations don't adequately compensate for the "Himalayan Distance Penalty" in implementation costs.

## 24.12 WAY FORWARD — STRATEGIC SOLUTIONS

| Solution | Implementation |
|---|---|
| **Restructure Pension Frameworks** | Shift toward contribution-based models; cap legacy pension growth |
| **Monetize Green Assets** | Carbon credits + Green Tourism Cess + GEP-linked climate adaptation fund |
| **Decentralized Cold-Chain Hubs** | Solar-powered cold storage in mountain clusters; curb 40% post-harvest loss |
| **Rebuild Cash Reserves** | Channel fixed % of revenue surplus into liquid investment accounts (target: 5% of revenue receipts) |
| **Strengthen ULB Own-Revenue** | GIS property mapping, self-certification, municipal bonds, Land Pooling Regulations 2025 |
| **Hill-Specific CapEx Formula** | Weight infrastructure allocations by terrain difficulty (Category A/B/C/D) not just population |
| **Expenditure Controls** | Freeze non-essential department expansion; multi-tasking cadres |
| **Digital Revenue Enhancement** | MDTSS for mining; BLIPUK for consumer-driven tax compliance; e-auction |
