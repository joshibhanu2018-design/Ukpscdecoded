## 18.5 ROAD TRANSPORT — ISSUES, CHALLENGES & WAY FORWARD

### Issues

| Challenge | Details |
|---|---|
| **Maintenance Burden** | **3–4× higher** than plains (Seismic Zones IV/V; landslides; GLOFs; "Sudden Asset Erosion") |
| **Ecological Fragility** | Heavy blasting + vertical slope cutting → destabilizes angle of repose → mass wasting |
| **Muck Dumping** | Unscientific disposal into river valleys → chokes drainage → raises riverbeds → flash flood risk |
| **Carrying Capacity Breach** | 7 crore tourists overload Nainital/Mussoorie → traffic paralysis |
| **Rising Accidents** | Treacherous roads + overloaded vehicles |

### Current Initiatives

| Initiative | Details |
|---|---|
| **iRAD** | Integrated Road Accident Database project |
| **HAMS Software** | Automated driver testing |
| **Rahgir Yojana** | Financial rewards to Good Samaritans helping accident victims in "Golden Hour" |
| **Green Road Engineering** | Cold Mix Technology + bio-engineering (coir geo-textiles + native grasses) to stabilize slopes |

### Way Forward

| Solution | Details |
|---|---|
| **Multi-Modal Integration** | Road base camps → Ropeways (Parvatmala) for last-mile eco-sensitive zones |
| **Green Roads** | Bio-engineering over heavy concrete |
| **Air-Bridge Strategy** | UDAN heli-taxi as monsoon alternatives |
| **Vibrant Villages** | Border road development linked to national security habitation |
| **Carrying Capacity Caps** | Strict daily vehicular entry limits for hill stations |

## 18.15 WATER SCARCITY — CAUSES & SOLUTIONS

### The Paradox
- **42% of India's water resources** originate here, yet **8,800 of 16,000 villages** face acute scarcity

### Causes

| Cause | Details |
|---|---|
| **Aquifer Puncturing** | Tunneling/mining severs underground veins permanently |
| **Climate Change** | 12% glacier area loss; chronic winter snow deficit → reduced spring recharge |
| **Deforestation** | Banj Oak → Chir Pine replacement reduces percolation |
| **Steep Topography** | Rapid surface runoff → limited groundwater recharge |
| **Urbanization** | Concretization of recharge zones (Joshimath case) |

### Solutions & Interventions

| Solution | Details |
|---|---|
| **SARA (Nov 2023)** | ₹125 crore; Springshed Management; maps exact recharge zones; ₹5 Cr each for Shipra (Nainital) + W. Nayar (Pauri) |
| **Bhagirath App (2025)** | Citizens map endangered water sources |
| **Chaal-Khal** | Traditional micro-ponds on ridges for rainwater trapping + aquifer recharge |
| **Naula/Dhara Restoration** | Ancient tiered stepwells and spouts revived |
| **Ecological Flow (e-flow)** | 20-30% minimum release mandated for all HEPs → sustains Golden Mahseer habitat |
| **Rooftop Rainwater Harvesting** | Mandatory for new buildings in Dehradun, Nainital |
| **"Gramya" Watershed** | Ridge-to-valley: check dams, contour trenches, artificial recharge structures |
| **Catch the Rain** | Campaign for awareness + infra |
| **Hydrum** | Hydraulic Ram — lifts water without electricity (hill-specific) |
