## 10.4 ANCIENT TEMPLE CLUSTERS — ASI RECOGNIZED MONUMENTS

> *Uttarakhand has 44 Monuments of National Importance recognized by ASI*

### Jageshwar Dham (Almora)

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| **Location** | Banks of Jata Ganga in dense deodar forest |
| **Temples** | Over **100 temples** (largest complex in state) |
| **Period** | 7th to 14th centuries |
| **Key Shrines** | Dandeshwar, Kuber Temple (god of wealth), Nau Durga (Nanda Devi) Temple |
| **Style** | Nagara and Shikhara |

### Dwarahat Group (Almora) — "Dwarika of the Himalayas" / "Khajuraho of Kumaon"

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| **Temple Groups** | Eight main groups |
| **Notable** | Gujardeo Temple (most artistic; finest Katyuri architecture), Kutumbari Temple ("lost" temple — components used by locals), Badrinath Group, Kacheri Group, Maniyan Group, Ratandeo Shrines, Mrityunjaya |
| **Style** | Indigenous Kumaoni architectural style |

### Katarmal Sun Temple (Almora)

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| **Built** | 12th century |
| **Distinction** | **2nd largest Sun Temple in India** (after Konark) |
| **Unique Feature** | Main idol faces east |
| **Historical Proof** | Proves Shaka dominance and spread of Sun worship in Kumaon |

### Baijnath Group (Bageshwar)

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| **Location** | Confluence of Gomti and Garud Ganga |
| **Built By** | King Bhudev (Katyuri dynasty) |
| **Famous As** | Marriage place of Lord Shiva and Parvati |
| **Baghnath Temple** | Only ancient Shiva temple in North India that **faces south** |

### Baleshwar Temples (Champawat)

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| **Distinction** | Exquisite stone carvings reflecting Chand dynasty influence |
| **Unique Feature** | Integrated **Naula** (covered spring) — traditional water conservation architecture |

### Lakha Mandal (Dehradun)

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| **Association** | Pandavas (Mahabharata) |
| **Known As** | "Storehouse of idols" |
| **Unique Feature** | Shiva Lingam believed to **reflect images** of those who pour water on it |

## 10.5 SPECIALIZED & TRIBAL SHRINES

| Temple | Location | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| **Mahasu Devta Temple** | Hanol, Dehradun | Main deity of **Jaunsari tribe**; famous for unique **judicial system** where deity delivers justice; other Mahasu temples at Barkot and Pujeli (Uttarkashi) |
| **Chitai Goljyu Temple** | Almora | Known as **'Supreme Court' of Uttarakhand**; devotees write letters seeking justice; deity depicted riding horse with bow and arrow |
| **Sem Mukhem Nagraja** | Tehri | 14th-century temple; God of Nagas; Indra Devta worshipped; signifies deep roots of **Naga worship** |
| **Ghatku Temple** | Champawat | Major centre of faith for tribal communities |
| **Dhari Devi** | Srinagar | Idol believed to **transform appearance** (girl → woman → old lady) as day progresses |
| **Raghunath Temple** | Devprayag | Dedicated to Lord Rama; architectural anomaly — built in **Dravidian style** (unique in this region) |
| **Tapkeshwar Mahadev** | Dehradun | Ancient cave temple; water naturally drips on Shiva Lingam; on Tons River banks |
| **Neelkanth Mahadev** | Pauri | Near Shambhu & Nishambhu mountains; major Shiva site |
| **Kamleshwar Temple** | Srinagar | Famous for **Baikunth Chaturdashi fair**; couples worship for children |

## 10.8 SOCIO-ECONOMIC & CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE (For Mains Analysis)

| Dimension | Role of Fairs & Festivals |
|---|---|
| **Preservation of Identity** | Primary platforms for endangered oral traditions (Jagar), dialects, folk arts |
| **Economic Hubs** | Historical trade fairs (Jauljibi, Gauchar) essential for barter and commerce; now promote ODOP & handicrafts |
| **Social Cohesion** | Resolve enmities (e.g., Gagli Yuddh Utsav in Jaunsar); inter-faith harmony (Piran Kaliyar) |
| **Challenges** | Ecological degradation at high-altitude sites; commercialization threatens authenticity |

---

## 10.9 GLOBALLY RECOGNIZED FESTIVALS

### Ramman (Ritual Theater of Chamoli)

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| **Location** | Saloor-Dungra villages, Chamoli |
| **Timing** | Annually in April-May (Baisakh) |
| **Recognition** | **UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity (2009)** |
| **Nature** | Ritual theater for village deity **Bhumiyal Devta**; mask dances; folk theater; Ramayana narratives |

### Kumbh Mela (The Global Spiritual Gathering)

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| **Location** | Haridwar |
| **Frequency** | Every 12 years (when Jupiter enters Aquarius, Sun enters Aries) |
| **UNESCO** | Included in **World Heritage List (2017)** |
| **Crowd** | Exceeds 30 lakhs at peak |
| **Infrastructure** | Haridwar Master Plan 2040; ₹1,027 crore budget allocation |

### Nanda Devi Raj Jat Yatra (The Himalayan Mahakumbh)

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| **Nature** | **World's longest religious pilgrimage** — 280 km on foot, 19-20 days |
| **Frequency** | Every 12 years |
| **Most Recent** | 2014; next planned for **2027** |
| **Start** | Nauti/Kansuwa (Chamoli) |
| **End** | Homkund |
| **Unique Feature** | Led by sacred **four-horned male sheep (Medha)** |

## 10.10 DEVI DHURA BAGWAL (THE STONE WAR)

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| **Location** | Varahi Devi Temple, Devidhura (Champawat) |
| **Timing** | Raksha Bandhan |
| **Tradition** | "Stone war" between 4 clans: **Chamyal, Valik, Gahadwal, Limgadiya** |
| **Participants Called** | **Dyoke** |
| **Modern Change (2013)** | Now played peacefully with flowers and fruits (to prevent injuries) |

---

## 10.11 HARELA (FESTIVAL OF GREENERY)

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| **Timing** | 1st day of Shravan month (monsoon) |
| **Significance** | Marks beginning of harvest season |
| **Ritual** | Residents plant barley & wheat in pots days in advance; offer grown greenery to deities |
| **Symbolism** | Prosperity and renewal; linked to environmental conservation |

## 10.12 OTHER MAJOR FAIRS & FESTIVALS — BY CATEGORY

### Trade Fairs

| Fair | Location | Key Facts |
|---|---|---|
| **Jauljibi Mela** | Pithoragarh | At confluence of Kali and Gauri rivers; since **1914**; key for Indo-Nepal trade |
| **Gauchar Mela** | Chamoli | Started **1943** by Commissioner Burnady; promotes regional commerce |
| **Somnath Fair** | Almora | Famous for buying and selling of **animals** |

### Agricultural & Seasonal Festivals

| Festival | Details |
|---|---|
| **Phuldei / Phool Sankranti** | 1st of Chaitra; children place flowers on doorsteps for prosperity |
| **Ghee (Olgiya) Sankranti** | Celebrated in Bhado; consuming ghee auspicious for all |
| **Khatarwa Festival** | Kumaoni festival for animals (cows); burning bonfires called **'Khatarduwa'** |
| **Makar Sankranti (Ghughutiya)** | Children fed **Ghughut** (deep-fried flour + jaggery) |
| **Harela** | 1st Shravan; greenery planting; harvest symbolism |
