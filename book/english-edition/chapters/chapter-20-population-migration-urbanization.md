### The "Spatial Concentration" Problem

> Over **50% of the population** resides in just THREE plain districts: Dehradun, Haridwar, and Udham Singh Nagar.

### The Sex Ratio Paradox

Hill districts show "artificially high" sex ratios (Almora 1139, Rudraprayag 1114) — NOT because of better gender equity, but because **working-age men have migrated out**, leaving women behind. This drives the **"Feminization of Hill Agriculture."**

## 20.5 MIGRATION (PALAAYAN) — COMPREHENSIVE ANALYSIS

### Magnitude

| Parameter | Data |
|---|---|
| **Household Level** | Nearly **1 in 3 households (27.8%)** has at least one migrant |
| **In Hill Districts** | Rises to **34.3%** |
| **Ghost Villages (2011)** | 1,034 with zero population |
| **Ghost Villages (RDMC 2018)** | **1,768** total (734 new since 2011) |
| **Daily Migration (2008-18)** | 138 people/day |
| **Daily Migration (2018-22)** | **193 people/day** |
| **Total (2018-22)** | 3.3 lakh migrated from villages |
| **Total (2008-18)** | 5.02 lakh; of which 1.1 lakh permanently shifted |

### Patterns & Characteristics

| Type | Data |
|---|---|
| **Short-term (<9 months)** | ~12% of migrants |
| **Long-term (>9 months)** | **85%** of all movement |
| **Permanent** | Creating Ghost Villages |
| **Inter-state destination** | 63.4% of households migrate to urban areas of other states (primarily Delhi, UP) |
| **Gender Shift** | Increasingly involves women + children for education/health |
| **Remittances** | **75.5%** of migrants remit money back ("Money Order Economy") |

### Primary Drivers (Push + Pull)

| Factor | % |
|---|---|
| **Employment search** (Push) | **38.93%** |
| **Better employment prospects** (Pull) | 11.94% |
| **Children's education** (Pull) | 9.01% |
| Other: Natural disasters, development displacement | — |

### Multi-Dimensional Impact

| Impact | Details |
|---|---|
| **Ghost Villages** | 1,768 deserted villages; aging population left behind |
| **Feminization of Agriculture** | Women = 60% of agri labour; 72% of water collection; earn 45% less than plains |
| **"Silver Economy"** | Aging demographic in hills — state training 1.5 lakh Care Sahayaks |
| **Strategic Vacuum** | Border districts (Pithoragarh, Champawat) depopulating — national security risk |
| **Political Marginalisation** | Hills losing demographic weight → future delimitation may reduce hill seats |
| **Gul/Van Panchayat Decay** | Traditional irrigation channels + forest councils unmaintained |

### Government Counter-Migration Initiatives

| Initiative | Details |
|---|---|
| **Vibrant Villages Programme** | 51 border villages (Mana, Gunji, Niti) → "First Villages" |

| **Silver Economy** | Ghost villages → Senior Living Communities; youth as Care Sahayaks |
| **Lakhpati Didi + House of Himalayas** | SHGs → formal market-linked enterprises |
| **VB-G RAM G** | 125 days guaranteed work; climate-resilient assets |
| **Pravasi Panchayat** | Started Tehri (April 2026); showcases successful self-employment stories |
| **Homestay Promotion** | Alternative livelihood for hill families |
| **CM Adarsh Gram (Sarkot)** | Model village; 2 per district target |
| **RDMC (Pauri-based)** | Renamed from "Migration Commission" → focus on reversing migration |

## 20.6 RURAL SETTLEMENT TYPES & PATTERNS

### Settlement Types

| Type | Location | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Nucleated/Compact** | Terai + Bhabar plains | Dense clustering on flat fertile land |
| **Dispersed/Isolated** | High-altitude zones | Scattered near individual terrace farms; minimizes travel on slopes |
| **Semi-dispersed** | Middle Himalayas | Small hamlets with moderate spacing |

### Geometric Patterns

| Pattern | Where Found | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Linear** | Along ridges/road-heads | Most dominant in hills; accessibility-driven |
| **Terraced/Contour** | Hill slopes | Follows contour lines; mirrors Sopan Krishi architecture |
| **Triangular/T-Shaped** | River confluences, road junctions | E.g., Devprayag (triangular at confluence) |
| **Circular** | Traditional tribal habitats | Defence-oriented; compact |

### Local Terminology

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Chhani / Bhunda** | High-altitude temporary dwellings for transhumance |
| **Gaon** | Permanent village |
| **Tola / Majra** | Sub-hamlet within a village |

| Challenge | Details |

| Fiscal Dependency | Most ULBs = near-zero Own-Tax Revenue; grant-dependent |
| "Development Tax" | Disasters periodically erase urban assets (3-4× maintenance vs plains) |

|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dehradun** | 0.765 |

| 3 | **US Nagar** | 0.717 |
| ... | ... | ... |
| 11 | Rudraprayag | 0.626 |
| 12 | Champawat | 0.620 |
| 13 | **Tehri Garhwal** | **0.611** (lowest) |

### The "Plains Paradox"

- Top 3 HDI districts are ALL in the plains
- They generate high GDP through industrialization (SIDCUL)
- BUT rank **lower** on SDG goals due to civic strain from rapid urbanization
- Hill districts lag in income levels despite better sex ratios and environmental quality
