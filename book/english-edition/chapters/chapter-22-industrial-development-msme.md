## 22.1 OVERVIEW: UTTARAKHAND'S INDUSTRIAL TRANSITION

The industrial sector in Uttarakhand has transitioned from a small public-sector base to a robust, **private-sector-led manufacturing hub**. The Secondary Sector now contributes **39.95%** to the GSDP (2025-26 estimate), with manufacturing alone accounting for **26.24%**.

| Parameter | At Formation (2000-01) | Current (2025) | Growth |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MSME Units** | 14,163 | **79,394–94,595** | ~6× |
| **MSME Employment** | ~40,000 | **4,56,605–4,63,478** | **~11×** |
| **Large Industries** | 39 | **107** | ~3× |
| **Exports** | Negligible | **₹14,952 Crore** | 400%↑ (since 2011-12) |
| **Startups** | 0 | **1,100–1,750** | — |
| **GI Tags** | 0 | **29** | — |

## 22.2 SIDCUL: THE INDUSTRIAL BACKBONE

### About SIDCUL

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| **Full Form** | State Infrastructure & Industrial Development Corporation of Uttarakhand Ltd |
| **Established** | **2002** (Government of Uttarakhand Enterprise) |
| **Authorized Share Capital** | ₹50 Crore |
| **Objective** | Overall industrial development and necessary infrastructure — directly or through Special Purpose Vehicles |
| **Role** | Nodal agency for industrial development in the state |

### 7 Integrated Industrial Estates (IIEs)

| # | IIE Name | Location (District) | Specialization |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **IIE Haridwar** | Haridwar | **Largest estate**; maximum industries; FMCG, Pharma |
| 2 | **IIE Pantnagar** | Udham Singh Nagar | Automobiles, Logistics hub |
| 3 | **IIE Selaqui** | Dehradun | Pharmaceuticals, Electronics |
| 4 | **IT Park, Sahastradhara** | Dehradun | Information Technology, Software |
| 5 | **Eldeco SIDCUL Industrial Park, Sitarganj** | Udham Singh Nagar | Various manufacturing units |
| 6 | **Sigaddi Growth Center** | Kotdwar (Pauri) | Strategic growth pole in Garhwal hills |
| 7 | **SIDCUL Phase 2, Sitarganj** | Udham Singh Nagar | Recent expansion |

### The Special Federal Package (2003)

The industrial boom was catalyzed by a **10-year Central excise tax holiday + income tax exemptions** (2003–2013) that attracted massive corporate investment to the plains. This converted Haridwar-Pantnagar-Selaqui into India's fastest-growing manufacturing corridor for:
- **Automobiles** (Tata Motors, Ashok Leyland, Mahindra, Hero MotoCorp)
- **FMCG** (HUL, Dabur, Nestlé, Patanjali)
- **Pharmaceuticals** (Cipla, Dr. Reddy's, Ranbaxy)

## 22.8 PROBLEMS OF INDUSTRIALIZATION — CRITICAL ANALYSIS

### Structural Issues

| Problem | Analysis |
|---|---|
| **Geographical Industrial Divide** | 80%+ industries in 3 plain districts; 10 hill districts largely excluded |
| **"Plains Paradox"** | Haridwar/US Nagar generate high GDP but rank LOWEST on SDG Index (13th/9th) due to civic strain |
| **Ecological & Seismic Fragility** | 90% in Seismic Zones IV & V; blasting/heavy engineering weakens MCT zones |
| **Logistical "Distance Penalty"** | No rail in high hills; winding single-lane roads; 3–4× maintenance cost vs plains |
| **Capital Flight (CD Ratio Crisis)** | Hill savings fund loans in plains; Bageshwar CD: 24.66%, Almora: 26.97% |
| **Brain Drain** | Productive age group migrates to plain-area industrial clusters |
| **PEVD Paradox** | CAG-flagged: poorly drafted DPRs → mid-way project abandonment |
| **Environmental Clearance Delays** | Forest diversion requirements + stringent EIA = project timeline slippage |
| **Employability Gap** | Traditional education ≠ modern tech industry demands (AI, IoT, Robotics) |
| **Human-Wildlife Conflict** | Disrupts industrial/agricultural activities in hill-fringe areas |
| **Traditional Craft Decline** | Chinese competition + labour migration → Almora copperware & carpet sector weakening |

## 22.9 MEGA INDUSTRIAL & INVESTMENT POLICY 2025 — PROVISIONS

### Classification of Enterprises

| Category | Capital Investment (excl. land) | Minimum Employment |
|---|---|---|
| **Large** | ₹50–200 Crore | 50 employees |
| **Ultra Large** | ₹200–500 Crore | 150 employees |
| **Mega** | ₹500–1,000 Crore | 300 employees |
| **Ultra Mega** | Above ₹1,000 Crore | 500 employees |

### Key Provisions

| Provision | Details |
|---|---|
| **Capital Subsidy** | Up to **20%** (max **₹400 Crore**) for Ultra Mega investments |
| **Hill Incentive** | Additional **1–2% capital subsidy** for industries in Category A & B mountain districts |
| **Stamp Duty** | 100% reimbursement for new units |
| **Excise Exemption** | 100% for 10 years |
| **Corporate Tax Exemption** | Up to 30% for new units |
| **Interest Subvention (3 years)** | Cat A: 3% (max ₹4L/yr); Cat B: 3% (max ₹5L/yr); Cat C: 3% (max ₹6L/yr) |
| **Priority Sectors** | Food processing, wine making, medicinal herbs, Pirul products |
| **Single Window** | Centralized CAF via investuttarakhand.com; 3–7 year execution timeline |
| **Flatted Factories** | 43,000 sq m plug-and-play space (Haridwar, Selakui, US Nagar) |
| **Worker Housing** | Rent-based accommodation via PPP in Haridwar, Kashipur, Pantnagar |

### Regional Categorization for Tourism/Industrial Subsidies

| Category | Districts/Areas |
|---|---|
| **Category A** | Haridwar (full), Nainital (full), US Nagar (full), parts of Dehradun & Almora |
| **Category B** | Kalsi, Chakrata, Tyuni (Dehradun); Garud (Bageshwar); Kotdwar, Lansdowne (Pauri); Dhanaulti (Tehri) |
| **Category C** | Entire Uttarkashi, Chamoli, Champawat, Rudraprayag, Pithoragarh + remaining tehsils of Bageshwar, Pauri, Tehri |
