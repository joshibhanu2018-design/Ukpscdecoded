### THE META-RULE (Golden Rule for quick answers)

```
PLAIN DISTRICTS (Haridwar, USN, Dehradun):
✓ HIGH: Population, Density, Growth, SC%, Agriculture
✗ LOW: Sex Ratio, Literacy (especially female)

HILL DISTRICTS (Almora, Pauri, Rudraprayag, Chamoli, Uttarkashi):
✓ HIGH: Sex Ratio, Forest Cover
✗ LOW: Population, Density, Growth (often negative)

EXCEPTIONS TO MEMORIZE:
- Nainital: HIGHEST forest % (not a typical plain or extreme hill)
- USN: HIGH population but LOWEST female literacy (anomaly)
- Dehradun: HIGHEST overall literacy (urban advantage)
- Uttarkashi: LOWEST density despite not being smallest area
- Chamoli: LARGEST area but very low population
```
