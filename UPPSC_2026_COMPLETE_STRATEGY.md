## PART 1: PRELIMS STRATEGY (Target: 6 Dec 2026)

### GS Paper I - Sources (One source per subject, max 2)

| Subject | PRIMARY Source | SECONDARY/MCQ Source |
|---------|---------------|---------------------|
| History (Modern) | Spectrum - A Brief History of Modern India | Ghatna Chakra History Module |
| History (Ancient/Medieval) | Lucent GK (Ancient + Medieval sections) | Old NCERT RS Sharma + Satish Chandra |
| Geography (India + World) | NCERT 11th & 12th | Ghatna Chakra Geography Module |
| Geography (UP specific) | Ghatna Chakra UP Tathya Saar 2026 | UP Census + Economic Survey |
| Polity | M. Laxmikanth (Indian Polity) | Ghatna Chakra Polity Module |
| Economy | Indian Economy - Ramesh Singh OR Sriram IAS Notes | NCERT 11th & 12th |
| Environment | Shankar IAS Environment | PYQ-based revision |
| Science & Tech | Lucent GK (Science section) | NCERT 6th-10th Science |
| Current Affairs | Drishti IAS Monthly Magazine + UP Daily CA | Ghatna Chakra Samsamayik |

### Key Strategy for Prelims:
1. **Target Score**: 110-120 out of 200 (attempt 120-130 Qs with 85%+ accuracy)
2. **PYQ Analysis**: Solve last 10 years UPPSC Prelims PYQs FIRST
3. **Mock Tests**: 100+ mocks minimum (Drishti/PW/TestBook series)
4. **Revision**: 3-5 revisions of notes/mind maps before exam
5. **UP GK in Prelims**: Significant weightage - don't ignore state-specific current affairs

## PART 2: GENERAL HINDI (Paper 1 - 150 Marks)

### Syllabus:
- Comprehension (Gadyansh)
- Precise Writing (Sankshiptan)
- Official/Semi-official Letter Writing
- Notice & Circular Drafting
- Word Knowledge (Prefix, Suffix, Antonyms, Synonyms, Spelling Correction)
- Idioms & Proverbs (Muhavare & Lokoktiyan)
- Vocabulary & Grammar

### Best Sources for Hindi Paper:

| Resource | Purpose | Priority |
|----------|---------|----------|
| **Vasundhara by Dr. Vijay (Hindi Bhasha)** | Grammar + Comprehension | PRIMARY |
| **Lucent General Hindi** | Quick grammar revision, idioms, proverbs | PRIMARY |
| **Ghatna Chakra Samanya Hindi** | MCQ practice + Previous year | HIGH |
| **Unique Publication Hindi** | Letter writing, official drafting | MEDIUM |
| **Previous Year Papers (10 years)** | Pattern understanding | ESSENTIAL |
| **UP Government Gazettes/Circulars** | Official language style | GOOD TO HAVE |

### Strategy:
- This is a **scoring paper** - most aspirants neglect it
- Focus on: Grammar rules, letter formats, precise writing practice
- Practice writing 1 letter + 1 precise daily for 30 days before mains
- Idioms/Proverbs: Make a list of 200 most common ones and memorize
- Scoring Potential: 110-120/150 easily achievable with practice

- Full Prelims revision mode
- 3-4 mocks per week
- PYQ solving (last 10 years complete)
- UP GK rapid revision

### Month 4 (October): Pre-Prelims Peak
- Daily mocks + error analysis
- Complete revision of all static subjects

- Only revision - NO new topics
