**[VISUAL: Show each maxim one by one with meaning — like a flashcard animation]**

Chaliye dekhte hain kya-kya pucha gaya:

**Question 45 — "Res Judicata" ka arth?**
Matlab — jo mamla pehle se decide ho chuka hai, use dobara court mein nahi la sakte. Answer: Jo mamla pehle hi nyayalay dwara faisla ho chuka hai.

**Question 49 — "Audi Alteram Partem" kis siddhant se related hai?**
Yeh NATURAL JUSTICE ka principle hai — "dusri side ko bhi suno". Answer: Prakritik Nyaya.

**Question 50 — "Ubi Jus Ibi Remedium"?**
"Jahan adhikar hai, wahan upchar hai" — Where there is a right, there is a remedy.

**Question 51 — "Res Ipsa Loquitur"?**
"Vastu swayam bolta hai" — The thing speaks for itself. Ye TORT law ka concept hai.

**Question 52 — "Volenti Non Fit Injuria"?**
"Sahmat vyakti ko kshati nahi karit hoti" — If you consented, you can't claim damage.

**Question 53 — "Mandamus" (Paramadesha) ka shabdik arth?**
"Hum aagya dete hain" — We Command. Ye ek Constitutional Writ hai jo Article 32 aur 226 ke under aati hai.

**[PAUSE — shift to analytical mode]**

**[VISUAL: Show comparison graphic — "Law Student: 8-9 correct out of 10" vs "General Aspirant: 4-5 correct out of 10"]**
