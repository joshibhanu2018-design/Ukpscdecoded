# UKPSC 2026 Exam Pattern & Complete Syllabus: Hindi & English Medium

This is the complete, official exam scheme and syllabus for the Uttarakhand Combined State Civil/Upper Subordinate Services Examination 2024-2026 — presented clearly for both Hindi and English medium aspirants.

Understanding the structure *before* you start preparing is non-negotiable. Most candidates waste their first 2-3 months studying the wrong things in the wrong proportions simply because they never sat down with the actual syllabus document.

---

## Exam Structure: Three Stages

The UKPSC Upper PCS exam has three sequential levels:

1. **Preliminary Examination** (Objective type) — Screening only
2. **Main Examination** (Written/Descriptive type) — Determines merit
3. **Interview/Personality Test** — Final selection

---

## Stage 1: Preliminary Examination (प्रारंभिक परीक्षा)

| Paper | Subject | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|-------|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| Paper 1 | General Studies (सामान्य अध्ययन) | 150 (1 mark each) | 150 | 2 hours |
| Paper 2 | General Aptitude Test / CSAT (सामान्य बुद्धिमत्ता परीक्षा) | 100 (1.5 marks each) | 150 | 2 hours |
| **Total** | | **250** | **300** | **4 hours** |

### Key Rules:
- **Negative marking**: 1/4th deduction for wrong answers
- **Merit list** is based on Paper 1 (General Studies) only
- **Paper 2 (CSAT) is qualifying** — minimum 33% required for all categories
- **Minimum 1/3rd questions** in Paper 1 will be from Uttarakhand state context

---

## Stage 2: Main Examination (मुख्य परीक्षा)


| Paper | Subject (विषय) | Duration | Marks |
|-------|----------------|----------|-------|
| 1 | General Hindi (सामान्य हिन्दी) | 3 hours | 150 |
| 2 | Essay (निबंध) | 3 hours | 150 |
| 3 | GS-I: Indian Heritage & Culture, World History & Geography, Society | 3 hours | 200 |
| 4 | GS-II: Governance, Constitution, Polity, Social Justice & International Relations | 3 hours | 200 |
| 5 | GS-III: Technology, Economic Development, Biodiversity, Environment, Security & Disaster Management | 3 hours | 200 |
| 6 | GS-IV: Ethics, Integrity and Aptitude (नीतिशास्त्र, सत्यनिष्ठा और अभिरुचि) | 3 hours | 200 |
| 7 | GS-V: Knowledge of State of Uttarakhand (उत्तराखण्ड राज्य की जानकारी) | 3 hours | 200 |
| 8 | GS-VI: Knowledge of State of Uttarakhand (उत्तराखण्ड राज्य की जानकारी) | 3 hours | 200 |
| **Written Total** | | | **1500** |

### Stage 3: Interview/Personality Test — 150 marks

**Grand Total (Written + Interview) = 1650 marks**

### Critical Notes:
- All papers are compulsory
- **Hindi paper**: Minimum 35% marks mandatory (otherwise disqualified)
- GS-V and GS-VI together carry **400 marks** — entirely Uttarakhand-specific
- Essay Section C is also Uttarakhand-specific (50 marks) — total state-specific weightage: **450+ marks**

## Prelims Paper 1: Complete Syllabus

### Unit 1: Indian History, Culture & National Movement (भारत का इतिहास, संस्कृति एवं राष्ट्रीय आन्दोलन)

**प्राचीन भारत / Ancient India:**
- Pre-Historic Period — Harappan Civilization, Vedic Civilization, Sangam Age
- Mahajanapadas and rise of Magadh
- Religious Movements — Jainism, Buddhism, Bhagavatism and Shaivism
- Persian and Greek contacts

**मौर्य साम्राज्य / Mauryan Empire:**
- Chandragupta Maurya, Ashoka and his Dhamma
- Mauryan Administration, Economy, Society and Art; Kushanas

**गुप्त साम्राज्य / Gupta Empire:**
- Foundation, Consolidation and Decline
- Chandragupta I, Samudragupta, Chandragupta II, Skandagupta
- Gupta Administration, Society, Economy, Literature and Art

**उत्तर-गुप्त काल / Post-Gupta Period:**
- Harshavardhan, Pal, Pratihara, Rashtrakuta, Chola, Pallava, Chandel, Paramar, Chauhan
- Social, Economic and Cultural Development (650-1200 AD)


**भारत में इस्लाम का आगमन / Advent of Islam:**
- Delhi Sultanate: Iltutmish, Balban, Alauddin Khalji, Muhammad bin Tughlaq, Feroze Tughlaq
- Administration, decline causes, Indo-Islamic Architecture, Vijayanagar Empire, Sufism, Bhakti Movement

**मुगल साम्राज्य / Mughal Empire:**
- Babar, Shershah Suri, Akbar, Shahjahan, Aurangzeb and decline
- Mughal administration, Jagirdari & Mansabdari systems
- Society, Economy, Literature, Art & Architecture; Maratha, Sikh and Jat

**ब्रिटिश काल / British Period:**
- Advent of Europeans — Portuguese, Dutch, French
- British East India Company and rule (1758-1857)
- Economic impact of British rule
- Socio-religious Reform Movements (19th century)
- Viceroys of India (1858-1947)
- First War of Independence (1857)
- Administrative, Social and Judicial systems after 1858

**राष्ट्रीय आन्दोलन / National Movement:**
- Indian National Congress — Foundation, Moderates and Extremists
- Bengal Partition, Swadeshi Movement, Muslim League, Surat Session (1907)
- Home Rule Movement, Lucknow Pact (1916), Rowlatt Act, Jallianwala Bagh
- Khilafat, Non-Cooperation, Swaraj Party, Simon Commission
- Civil Disobedience, Round Table Conferences, Quit India Movement
- Cabinet Mission, INA, Indian Independence Act (1947), Partition

**उत्तराखण्ड का इतिहास एवं संस्कृति / History & Culture of Uttarakhand:**
- Prehistoric & Proto-historic periods
- Ancient tribes of Uttarakhand
- Kuninda and Yaudheya
- Kartikepur dynasty, Katyuri dynasty
- Parmar dynasty of Garhwal; Chand dynasty of Kumaon
- Gorkha invasion and rule
- British Rule; Tehri Estate
- Freedom Movement — 1857 and Uttarakhand's role
- People's Movements of Uttarakhand

**विश्व का भूगोल / World Geography:**

- Drainage system, Vegetation, Soils, Water Resources
- Agriculture, Minerals, Industries
- Population & Urbanization, Transport, Foreign Trade
- Scheduled Castes/Tribes, Pollution


**उत्तराखण्ड का भूगोल / Geography of Uttarakhand:**
- Geographical location, Relief & Structure, Climate
- Drainage system, Vegetation, Wildlife, Minerals
- Agriculture, Animal Husbandry, Irrigation
- Major Cities, Tourist Places, Population
- SC/ST, Transport Network, Power Resources

**भारतीय राजनीति / Indian Polity:**

**पंचायती राज / Panchayati Raj:**

## Prelims Paper 2 (CSAT): Syllabus

### Unit 1 (80 questions, 120 marks):
1. **Aptitude**: Statements, Syllogism, Analogies, Similarities
2. **Communication**: Word building, Coding-Decoding, Numerical Operations
3. **Logical & Analytical ability**: Arguments, Assumptions, Venn Diagrams, Number Series
4. **Decision-making**: Problem solving, Visual memory, Assertion & Reason
5. **General Mental Ability**: Direction sense, Social/Emotional Intelligence, Critical thinking
6. **Numerical Identification**: Divisibility, Prime numbers, Remainder rules
7. **Statistical Analysis**: Charts, Graphs, Tables, Data sufficiency

### Unit 2 (20 questions, 30 marks):
8. **English Comprehension**: Long passage + 3 questions; Grammar (Vocabulary, Antonyms, Synonyms, Phrases, Transformation)
9. **Hindi Comprehension (हिन्दी बोधगम्यता)**: Unseen passage + 6 questions on content, language & grammar; 7 questions on idioms, figures of speech, word types (तत्सम, तद्भव, देशज, विदेशी), prefixes, suffixes, sandhi, samas

## Strategy Note for Hindi Medium Students (हिन्दी माध्यम के छात्रों के लिए)

1. **हिन्दी पेपर को हल्के में न लें** — यह qualifying नहीं, scoring है। 35% minimum + rank-deciding marks यहीं से आते हैं
2. **उत्तराखण्ड विशिष्ट** — GS-V और GS-VI के लिए S.K. Pandey, Khadakwal, Himanshu Kapatia पढ़ें
3. **निबंध** — Section C (उत्तराखण्ड) को सबसे पहले तैयार करें — 50 marks guaranteed scoring area
4. **Current Affairs** — Winsar Publication + Ghatnachakra (दोनों Hindi में उपलब्ध)
5. **NCERT Hindi medium** में उपलब्ध हैं — Class 6-12 History, Geography, Polity सबसे पहले पढ़ें

## Strategy Note for English Medium Students

1. **Don't skip the Hindi paper** — it's compulsory and scoring (35% minimum mandatory)
2. **Uttarakhand-specific papers**: Use Ajay Rawat (History), Khadakwal (Geography), Arihant's UK GK
3. **Essay Section C**: Prepare Uttarakhand topics specifically — tourism, migration, disaster management, women empowerment
4. **Current Affairs**: Winsar Publication (available in English) + Economic Survey of Uttarakhand
5. **NCERTs in English** (6-12) for History, Geography; Laxmikanth for Polity; Tamil Nadu 11th History as alternative

## Key Takeaway: Where the Marks Actually Are

| Component | Marks | % of Total |
|-----------|-------|------------|
| Uttarakhand Papers (GS-V + GS-VI) | 400 | 24.2% |
| Essay Section C (Uttarakhand) | 50 | 3.0% |
| Hindi Paper | 150 | 9.1% |
| National GS (I + II + III + IV) | 800 | 48.5% |
| Essay Sections A + B | 100 | 6.1% |
| Interview | 150 | 9.1% |
| **Total** | **1650** | **100%** |

**The bottom line**: Uttarakhand-specific preparation alone controls 450+ marks (27%+ of selection). Combined with Hindi (another 150), state-level preparation influences nearly 600/1650 marks — more than one-third of your final score.
