### 🎥 MY METHOD — 3 Rules

Meri teen rules hain.

Rule number one.

EK topic — EK page.

Zyaada nahi.

Agar ek page mein fit nahi hota —

Toh tumne filter nahi kiya hai.

Sab kuch likhna zaruri nahi.

Sirf woh likho jo YAAD nahi rehta.

Rule number two.

Keywords. Full sentences nahi.

Main kabhi poora sentence nahi likhta.

Rule number three.

Har hafte revise karo.

Bina revision ke notes bhi kitaab ban jaate hain.

Dhool khaate hain.

30 minutes. Poora syllabus ek baar.
